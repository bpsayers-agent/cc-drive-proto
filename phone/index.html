Method

Steps
  1. Type which phone this is above. Choose method A.
  2. In Safari: tap Sign in, finish Google's screens. Tap Check now. The Drive check should show your account.
  3. Share button → Add to Home Screen. Close Safari.
  4. Open Token test from the home screen. The badge should say Home-screen app. Sign in, then Check now.
  5. Optional quick try: tap Pretend expired and note what happens.
  6. Close the app (swipe it away). Wait over an hour, overnight if you can.
  7. Open it from the home screen. Touch nothing for 10 seconds. Did it renew by itself? If not, tap Renew. Answer "What did you see?"
  8. Choose method B and repeat steps 2 to 7.
  9. Tap Copy log and paste it into a message to Ben.

Pass = renewal happens by itself or with one tap. A fail is useful too: it just means use it as a Safari tab.
